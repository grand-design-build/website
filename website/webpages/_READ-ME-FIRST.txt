DO NOT OPEN THE .html FILES IN THIS FOLDER DIRECTLY.

They are sources, not pages. The design, the menu, the footer and the
photographs are all added when the site is built. Opened on their own they
look like plain black text on white with broken images -- nothing is wrong,
you are just looking at the ingredients instead of the meal.

TO LOOK AT THE WEBSITE:

    Go up two folders to GDB and double-click  preview.command

That rebuilds the site and opens it in your browser. Leave the Terminal
window open while you are looking at it, and close it when you are done.

Edit the files in here, save, then double-click preview.command again to see
the change.
