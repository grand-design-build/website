The Process

Build a laneway house in Toronto

Laneway housing transforms underused backyard space into a modern, self-contained home that generates rental income, increases long-term property value, and provides comfortable housing for family members — all while staying within your community. With strong support from Toronto’s planning policies, approvals and permits are now more accessible than ever.

Requirements

Toronto Laneway House Requirements

Laneway housing is actively supported by Toronto’s planning policies, making it easier than ever to get approvals and permits. These are the conditions your property and design need to meet.

Public Laneway Access

Your property must back onto a public laneway to qualify for a laneway suite.

Zoning & Lot Requirements

The lot must meet minimum size standards and comply with City of Toronto zoning by-laws.

Height, Size & Setbacks

The suite must follow restrictions on height, total floor area, and maintain required separation from the main house.

Building Code Compliance

The design must meet Ontario Building Code standards for structure, safety, and habitability.

Building Permit & Plans

A building permit is mandatory, supported by detailed architectural and technical drawings.

Fire Safety & Emergency Access

A clear path to a public street is required to ensure emergency services can access the unit.

Landscaping & Tree Protection

Soft landscaping requirements must be maintained, and any impact on trees requires Urban Forestry approval.
