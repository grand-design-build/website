Questions

FAQ

How is your fixed-price model different from the cost-plus model most Toronto builders use?

Most high-end builders work on a cost-plus basis, where you pay the actual cost of labour and materials plus a fee on top — the financial risk is yours. Grand Design Build prices the full defined scope before we break ground, so if a cost shifts during construction, that's our risk to manage, not yours.

What does it cost to build or renovate a home in Toronto?

Cost depends on lot conditions, square footage, finish level, and scope. We offer a detailed, itemized quote within 48 hours of our first conversation, broken down line by line.

How long does it take to build a house in the GTA?

A straightforward new build typically runs six to twelve months once permits are approved; a fully custom home tends to run twelve to eighteen months, largely due to design and permitting time.

Do you handle laneway or garden suites from start to finish?

Yes — the structural, utility, and permit requirements specific to Toronto's laneway and garden suite bylaws, from initial design through to final construction.

Are you licensed, and what protection does that give me?

Licensed by the HCRA and fully insured across every project — backed by our own seven-year structural guarantee.

Which neighbourhoods and areas do you build in?

A strong concentration in North York, Forest Hill, Rosedale, Bloor West Village, Leaside, The Beaches, and the surrounding central Toronto neighbourhoods, along with wider GTA municipalities.
