The short version

  • ~1,450 words down to ~640. Almost the entire cut is guarantee preamble and testimonial length. No section of the live page is lost except "We Show Up" — and that copy is preserved inside Our Story rather than deleted.
  • The four guarantees run ~95 words each on live, and each one opens with preamble ("At Grand Design Build, your safety and peace of mind come first…") before reaching the actual promise. Trimmed to ~28 words each; every promise kept, nothing reworded into new claims.
  • Seven testimonials cut to three. The full versions stay on Google — link out rather than reprint 620 words of them.
  • The live H1 is just "About Us" — a wasted H1 on the page that ranks for brand plus "Toronto custom home builder." Recommended replacement below, with the mockup's version as the alternative.
  • The mockup adds one section the live page doesn't have at all: Meet the Team. Flagged — and I'd hold it back from publish until real headshots exist.
  • Two numbers need confirming before publish: "15+ Years / since 2010" and "250+ Projects." Both come from the homepage draft, not the live About page, where they were flagged as new copy too.
  • Nothing here touches WordPress. Copy only, for whoever's building the Elementor template.
