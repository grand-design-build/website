How It Started

Our Story

Allen Misaghi didn't build Grand Design Build overnight. He built it the hard way, through relentless dedication, an uncompromising eye for quality, and a refusal to settle for anything less than exceptional.

What started as one man's pursuit of excellence grew into one of Toronto's most recognized names in construction and architectural development. Project by project. Client by client. Building by building.

"This isn't just a company. It's what never stopping looks like."

On every jobsite. For every client. Through every challenge. That's not a slogan — that's how Grand Design Build was built, and it's how we operate every day.
